Why Cash on Delivery Is Essential in Pakistan's Online Shopping

Trust in online transactions is still building in Pakistan. Credit and debit card penetration is limited, and the fear of not receiving products as described is real. Cash on delivery (COD) solves this problem completely — you pay only when the product arrives at your door and you're satisfied.

Any serious Pakistani fashion brand should offer COD. It's not just a payment option — it's a statement of confidence in your own product quality.
