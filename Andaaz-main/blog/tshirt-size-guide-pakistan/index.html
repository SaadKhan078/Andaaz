T-Shirt Size Guide Pakistan — How to Choose the Right Fit

By Andaaz Team · 2026-05-28 · 4 min read

Buying the wrong size online is frustrating — but completely avoidable. Here's exactly how to pick your perfect t-shirt size in Pakistan.

The Problem With T-Shirt Sizing in Pakistan

Pakistan's t-shirt market has no standardized sizing. An M from one brand fits like an L from another. An "oversized" label can mean anything from slightly large to dramatically oversized. This inconsistency is the #1 cause of returns and exchanges in online fashion.

The solution: always buy by measurements, not by label.

How to Measure Yourself in 30 Seconds

You need a soft measuring tape (or a string and a ruler). Measure in inches for Pakistani fashion brands.

Chest Measurement

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms. Keep it horizontal and snug but not tight. This is your chest measurement.

That's Actually All You Need

For most t-shirt purchases, chest measurement alone is sufficient. Length and shoulder width follow from chest size in quality brands that size consistently.
