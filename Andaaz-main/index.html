Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes does Andaaz offer?

Andaaz t-shirts are available in S, M, L, and XL. All our fits are oversized — if you prefer a fitted look, size down by one.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery across Pakistan?

Yes! Cash on Delivery available in all major cities — Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta, Faisalabad, Multan, and more.

How do I place an order?

Add to cart and click "Order on WhatsApp" — or WhatsApp us at 0331-0214667. We respond 7 days a week, 9 AM – 10 PM.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across Pakistan. Karachi orders may arrive sooner. Free delivery on orders above Rs 2,000.

What is your return policy?

7-day hassle-free returns. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags. WhatsApp us to start — return shipping free for defective items.

What fabric does Andaaz use?

100% combed cotton — soft, breathable, maintains shape and print quality through multiple washes.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within Pakistan. For international orders (UAE, UK, USA), contact us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange it.
