In Karachi, fashion moves fast. The city's 15+ million residents include some of Pakistan's most fashion-conscious young people — and the trend they've collectively adopted most enthusiastically is the oversized t-shirt.

Karachi's climate — hot for most of the year, humid near the coast — makes loose, breathable clothing practically necessary. The oversized t-shirt perfectly answers this need: airy, comfortable, and easy to move in while still looking intentional.
